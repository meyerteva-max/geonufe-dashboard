GeoNuFE Observatory of Nuclear Fuel Supplies
Getting started with GeoNuFE
📍
Click any site on the map to see its owner(s), capacity, and any notes.
⚡
Quick view — the top row of chips jumps straight to a full stage of the fuel chain (mining, conversion, enrichment, fuel fabrication, or power plants).
🎛️
Customize — the row below lets you combine any facility types, flow materials, and a production-share overlay, plus filter by country, owner, or year.
➡️
Flow arrows point from the exporting country to the importing country's center — thicker arrows mean larger volumes.
📊
Statistics panel — click the tab on the right edge to see capacity-share charts; drag its edge to resize.
🔍
Drag the map to pan, use the +/− buttons or scroll to zoom, and FIT to reset the view.
