GeoNuFE Observatory of Nuclear Fuel Supplies
Getting started with GeoNuFE
📍
Click any site on the map to see its owner(s), status, capacity, and any notes. Symbol area is proportional to capacity within each stage of the chain.
⚡
Quick view — the top row of chips jumps straight to a full stage of the fuel chain (mining, conversion, enrichment, fuel fabrication, or power plants).
🎛️
Customize — combine facility types, status (operating, suspended, under construction, planned), flow materials and a production-share overlay, plus filter by country or owner.
➡️
Flow arrows point from the exporting country to the importing country's center — thicker arrows mean larger volumes. Use the year slider to change the flow year.
📊
Statistics panel — click the tab on the right edge to see capacity shares (operating sites only) and, when a country is selected, a country summary; drag its edge to resize.
🔍
Drag the map to pan, use the +/− buttons or scroll to zoom, and FIT to reset the view.
